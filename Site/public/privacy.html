Privacy

Last updated November 23, 2026.

What we store

  • Your email address, so we can confirm your spot and invite you when Widgie is ready.
  • Which page you signed up from (partners or personal growth), if any.
  • The site or channel that sent you, if the link said so.

What we don't do

  • No ad trackers and no cookies. Visit counts come from Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not identify you.
  • We never sell or share your email. It is used only for Widgie updates and your invite to the app.

Who handles it

The list lives in a Cloudflare database. Emails are sent through Resend. Bot checks use Cloudflare Turnstile.

Leaving

Every email has a link to remove yourself, which deletes your row. You can also reply to any Widgie email and ask.
